Talkie Fiber Speed Test privacy policy

Talkie Communications · Last updated: October 19, 2026

Talkie Fiber Speed Test is an internet speed test app from Talkie Communications. This page explains what the app does with information. In short: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and your results stay on your device.

What the app does

When you tap GO, the app sends and receives test data between your device and the Talkie Communications speed test servers. It measures how long this takes (ping and jitter) and how much data moves per second (download and upload speed). The test data is random filler, not your files. If a Talkie test server does not answer, the app uses Cloudflare's public speed test servers (speed.cloudflare.com) as a backup.

Information the app handles

  • Your IP address. Like any internet connection, the Talkie test servers see your IP address while the test runs. Talkie Communications uses it only to carry out the test and does not link it to an account, because the app has no accounts.
  • Connection details. To show your provider, approximate area and IP address on screen, the app asks Cloudflare's public lookup service. Cloudflare sees your IP address as part of that request.
  • Test results. Your speeds, ping, jitter and the time of each test are saved only on your device (in the app's private storage) so the History tab can show them. They are never uploaded to Talkie Communications. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.
  • Connection type. The app checks whether you are on Wi‑Fi or mobile data, to show it next to your result and to warn you about data use. This stays on your device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • It does not show ads and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not sell or share personal information.

Sharing

The Share button opens your phone's share sheet with a short text of your result. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Cloudflare

Cloudflare provides the connection details lookup and the backup test servers, and handles the data it receives under its own privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Data use

A speed test downloads and uploads data for about ten seconds each way and can use hundreds of megabytes on a fast connection. Use Wi‑Fi if your mobile plan is limited.

Children

The app is a general utility. It is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown here with a new date.
